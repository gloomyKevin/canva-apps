Privacy policy

Effective 9 September 2026

This policy covers the Tategaki app for the Canva Apps Marketplace. It is short because the app collects nothing.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no server, no database and no analytics. It sends no requests to the developer or to any third party. The developer receives no personal information, no usage statistics, no text you type and no images you create, and therefore has nothing to store, share, sell or disclose.

What happens to your text

The text you type into the panel is laid out and drawn entirely inside the app panel in your browser, using the fonts installed on your own device. It is not transmitted anywhere while you are editing.

When you press Insert into design, the app hands the finished image to Canva through Canva's own app interfaces so that it can be placed in your design. From that point the image is part of your Canva design and is handled by Canva under Canva's privacy policy. The developer is not a recipient of that data and has no access to it.

Permissions the app requests

  • canva:design:content:read — needed to place an element at the current position in your design.
  • canva:design:content:write — needed to insert the rendered image.

The app requests no other permissions. It does not read your designs for any purpose other than inserting the element you asked for, and it does not access your account, your brand assets or your folders.

Cookies and local storage

The app sets no cookies and writes nothing to local storage, session storage or any in-browser database. Your settings are not remembered between sessions.

Children

The app is a design tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no information at all, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address on the support page.
