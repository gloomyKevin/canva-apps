Tategaki

An app for the Canva Apps Marketplace

Tategaki sets Japanese and Chinese text in vertical columns that run from top to bottom and read right to left, then inserts the result into your Canva design as a transparent image.

What it does

  • Vertical and horizontal writing modes. Columns run right to left, the way vertical Japanese and Chinese text is set.
  • Line breaking rules. A full stop or comma is never left at the start of a line, and an opening bracket is never left at the end.
  • Punctuation spacing. The extra half space inside full width punctuation can be squeezed out.
  • Spacing around Latin text. A quarter space is added between Japanese or Chinese characters and adjacent letters or numbers.
  • Upright short numbers. In vertical text, one and two digit numbers stay upright instead of rotating.
  • Punctuation placement. Full stops and commas sit in the top right corner of their own square, and brackets rotate to follow the column.

How it works

Everything runs inside the app panel in your browser. The text is laid out on a canvas using the fonts already installed on your device, and the finished image is added to your design. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

Because the app uses your device fonts, the same text can look slightly different on a different computer. The panel always tells you which font is actually being used.
